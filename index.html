Each indicator you log earns 10 points. Log at least one reading a day to keep your streak.

Health reminders

Regular alerts to drink water, take supplements, exercise, measure vitals, check your mood, and rest. Set each interval in minutes. Follow your flight surgeon’s plan for supplements. Keep this tab open to receive alerts; a short beep sounds when one is due. Marking one done earns 5 points.

Mind and connection

Long missions can feel lonely, and mental health is easy to neglect. These small daily habits protect it. Everything here stays on this device unless you press the support button.

My motivation
